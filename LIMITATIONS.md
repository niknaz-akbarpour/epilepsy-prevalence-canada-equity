# Limitations and interpretation safeguards

1. **Administrative case definitions:** CCDSS identifies people who meet a surveillance case definition based on administrative records. This does not independently confirm a clinical diagnosis.
2. **Ever-identified versus active epilepsy:** The two measures answer different questions and are reported separately.
3. **No national race-stratified estimate:** CCDSS does not provide race, ethnicity, Indigenous identity or immigration status in the public epilepsy table.
4. **Do not substitute incidence for prevalence:** The Saskatchewan Indigenous-status comparison is an incidence estimate from 2005–2010 and cannot be plotted as national prevalence.
5. **Small-cell suppression:** Estimates are not reported for counts below 10 or coefficients of variation above 33.3%.
6. **Random rounding:** Crude counts are randomly rounded to an adjacent multiple of five.
7. **Pandemic period:** Values from 2020–2021 through 2022–2023 may reflect changes in care-seeking and service availability as well as health status.
8. **Provincial comparability:** Historical completeness and coding changes vary across jurisdictions.
9. **Race is not a biological causal variable:** Any future disparity analysis must consider structural, socioeconomic, environmental and health-care access factors.
